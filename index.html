Privacy Policy — M Mixing

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Developer: AOF3D

1. Introduction

M Mixing is an iPad application developed by AOF3D to remotely control compatible M32 digital mixing consoles. It provides a redesigned mixing interface, Custom Fader functionality, and real-time audio analysis through Mic RTA.

2. Information Collection

M Mixing does not require an account or sign-in. The application is designed to operate over a local network for its core mixing control functions.

The developer does not intentionally collect or transmit users' personal information to the developer or third parties through the application's described core functionality.

3. Microphone and Audio Processing

M Mixing requests microphone permission when a microphone-dependent feature is first used. Mic RTA processes audio locally on the iPad to provide real-time sound analysis.

Compatible external audio interfaces may also be used as audio input sources, subject to device and application compatibility. The application does not retain a history of Mic RTA measurements or intentionally upload microphone audio for remote analysis.

Measurement accuracy may vary depending on the microphone or audio interface, device characteristics, placement, environmental conditions, and obstructions.

Users can manage microphone permission through the privacy settings on their Apple device.

4. Local Network and Wi-Fi

M Mixing communicates with compatible M32 mixing consoles over a local network. The application also provides Wi-Fi selection functionality to support network connectivity.

Network access is used to provide the application's connection and control features. The application is not designed to transmit network information to the developer or third parties for tracking or advertising.

5. Local Storage

Custom Fader configurations and application-specific settings are stored locally on the iPad. Scenes saved through the mixer's own scene-management functionality are stored on the mixer itself.

These functions do not require a developer-operated cloud service. Locally stored application data may be affected by application removal, device settings, or backup behavior.

6. Analytics and Third-Party Services

The developer does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs to collect or transmit user information, based on the application's current implementation as described by the developer.

7. Data Sharing

M Mixing does not intentionally sell, share, or transmit personal information to third parties. Its core functions are designed to process audio locally and communicate with compatible equipment over a local network.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Mic RTA measurement history is not retained by the application. Custom Fader configurations and other application-specific settings are stored locally on the iPad. Mixer scenes saved through the mixer's own scene-management functions remain on the mixer.

Removing the application may remove locally stored application data, subject to iPadOS and device backup behavior.

9. Children's Privacy

M Mixing is a general-purpose audio utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

10. Security

Users should maintain appropriate security on their devices, Wi-Fi networks, and connected audio equipment. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
